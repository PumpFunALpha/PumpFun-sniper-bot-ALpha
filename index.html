Getting started

WHAT IS PUMPFUN SNIPER?
PumpFun Sniper is a non-custodial Solana sniper bot that watches pump.fun (and soon Raydium / Base / Blast) for brand-new token launches, scores them with an AI rugpull shield, and — if the verdict is PASS — executes a buy from your own wallet in under 10 ms. You stay in full control of your keys at all times.

1. LAUNCH THE TERMINAL

Open the in-browser trading terminal at /terminal/. No download required, nothing to install. The terminal runs entirely client-side and talks to public Solana RPCs.

2. CONNECT A WALLET

Click Connect Wallet — choose Phantom, Solflare or Sollet. The extension pops up and asks you to approve read-only connection. We never receive your private keys.

3. FUND THE WALLET

Send at least 2 SOL to your sniping wallet. This gives the bot enough ammo to ride a realistic sample of trades without running dry on the first few bad picks.

4. PICK A PRESET

Balanced (default) / Aggressive / Safe — set slippage, take-profit, max dev-hold, rugpull shield level and AI agent with one click. You can always edit these in Settings.

5. PRESS START

Hit Start Trading in the Bot Control card. The bot scans 500+ tokens per minute, shows live analysis in the Logs tab and executes buys automatically when the AI agent returns PASS.

Wallet & funding

SUPPORTED WALLETS: Phantom, Solflare, Sollet, Backpack, and any wallet that implements the Solana Wallet Adapter spec.
